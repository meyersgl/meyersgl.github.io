## Question
After *Trump v. Slaughter* (2026), heads of executive agencies can be removed at will. How should the platform's commissions, funds, and watchdogs be designed?

## Decision
Take discretion away rather than trying to protect the people who exercise it. Use five tools: legislative-branch placement for advisory and scoring work; formulas written into statute; published, reasoned decisions; protections for career staff and inferior officers; and enforcement through the courts and independent third parties. Each body's design is listed in Appendix C.

## Why
Protecting agency heads from removal is no longer reliable for executive agencies. Rules written into statute, together with public reasoning, still constrain whoever holds the office.
