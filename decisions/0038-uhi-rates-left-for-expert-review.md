## Question
The trust-fund tests (Appendix E.4) show that comparable designs would add about $32.6 to $34 trillion to federal spending over ten years, but the platform sets no Universal Health Insurance contribution rates. Should the platform propose rates now?

## Decision
No. Contribution rates are left for independent scoring and expert review in the Universal Health Insurance white paper. The white paper now states the scale of the financing question, the constraints any design must meet (the 60 percent wage ceiling, no General Fund support, eldercare included, savings lower rates), and specific questions for reviewers.

## Why
Rates set before independent scoring would be guesses presented as commitments. The UHI financing design is the platform's largest and most technical question, and the one where expert review adds the most.

## Trade-off
Until rates are set, the platform cannot show that UHI balances, and Appendix E.4 reports it as "not yet demonstrable." Critics will say the largest program has no financing plan.
