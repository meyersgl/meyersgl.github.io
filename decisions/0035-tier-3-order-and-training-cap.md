## Question
After decision 0034, the conservative case funds tier 1 and tier 2 through preschool, but childcare caps wait. Because no tier may start before every earlier step is funded, about $500 billion of wealth-tax transfer, which may pay only for tiers 3 and 4, would sit unused. Separately, the training cap in Section 3.6.9 had no amount.

## Decision
- **D1, B.** A narrow wealth-tax exception in Section 27.9: if a tier 2 step waits only because stable revenue cannot cover it, the smoothed transfer may pay for tier 3 steps in order, as long as the transfer alone covers each step's full cost. Stable revenue still goes to the waiting tier 2 step first, and the transfer never pays for tiers 1 and 2. Tier 3 is reordered: K-12 equity funding, then tuition-free public higher education, then student-loan reform.
- **D2, B.** The shared training and apprenticeship cap is $5 billion a year, indexed for inflation, about $55 billion over ten years.

## Why
- Without the reorder, the exception would free nothing in the conservative case: tuition-free college ($700 to $800 billion) does not fit in a $500 billion transfer, but K-12 equity ($360 to $400 billion) does.
- K-12 equity funding is targeted to high-poverty districts; tuition-free college benefits many higher-income families.
- A $5 billion cap is several times today's main federal training funding for displaced workers and still leaves room for preschool in the conservative case.

## Options considered
- D1: keep the strict tier order (A); the exception with K-12 first (B, chosen); the exception with college first (C), which frees nothing in the conservative case.
- D2: $3 billion (A), $5 billion indexed (B, chosen), or $10 billion (C) a year.

## Trade-offs
Critics can say the platform funds tier 3 schools while tier 2 childcare waits. The answer is that childcare is paid from stable revenue, which the wealth tax was never allowed to replace. The transfer also arrives late in the ten-year window in the conservative case, so K-12 equity would start late.

## Supersedes
The tier 3 order in decisions 0023 (D9: college third) and 0030 (college first).
