## Question
How does the methane fee treat agriculture?

## Decision
Fee applies to oil and gas, landfills, and manure lagoons at the largest confined feeding operations. Livestock emissions and family farms are handled through paid voluntary incentives.

## Why
Consistent with family-farm protections and voluntary conservation.
