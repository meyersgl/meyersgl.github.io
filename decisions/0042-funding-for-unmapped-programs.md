## Question
Section 7.4 and Chapters 14, 17, and 22 had programs with no funding source in Section 27.4.

## Options considered
1. Existing appropriations only.
2. New fixed-budget programs with ceilings.
3. Capital costs through the Capital Investment Account and productive borrowing; operations within existing appropriations (chosen).

## Decision
- **One-time capital costs** (disaster mitigation construction, the initial build-out of emergency reserves, resilience improvements in reconstruction, and replacement of long-lived federal systems) use the Capital Investment Account and productive borrowing (Sections 27.5 and 27.7).
- **Operations** use existing authorized appropriations, and for immigration processing the existing fee accounts, with no new appropriation and no Section 27.9 phase-in obligation.
- Both rules are added to the Section 27.4 table and stated in funding-role notes in Chapters 14, 17, and 22 and in Section 7.4.

## Why
This matches Chapter 27's rule that borrowing funds only long-lived assets, and it adds no claim on any revenue stream.

## Trade-off
Mitigation and system replacement wait until the Sovereign Wealth Reserve reaches its target. Operating programs depend on annual appropriations. IT systems qualify for capital funding only where costing shows a long-lived asset.
