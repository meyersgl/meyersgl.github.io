## Question
Where is AI oversight housed, and does federal law preempt states?

## Decision
Commerce/NIST with statutory triggers and deadlines. Federal law is a floor; one national incident-reporting standard preempts conflicting state reporting rules.

## Why
Durable after Trump v. Slaughter; reduces compliance conflict without blocking state safeguards.
