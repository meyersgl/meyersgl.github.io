## Question
Keep offshore balancing as a core commitment, or narrow it?

## Decision
End regime-change operations (core). No new permanent overseas bases without congressional authorization. Alliance burden-sharing reviews and a periodic force-posture report to Congress.

## Why
Consistent with alliance and defense chapters; claims no unscored savings; hard to attack as abandoning allies.
