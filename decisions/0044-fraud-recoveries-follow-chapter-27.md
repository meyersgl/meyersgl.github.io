## Question
Section 14.4 said recovered funds go to "taxpayers or the originating program," while Section 27.3 says recovered fraud and improper payments return to the program they came from and are not counted as platform revenue. Which governs?

## Options considered
1. Amend Chapter 27 to allow refunds to taxpayers.
2. Align Section 14.4 with Section 27.3 (chosen).

## Decision
Section 14.4 now says recovered funds are returned to the program they came from and are not counted as platform revenue (Section 27.3).

## Why
Chapter 27 is the single authority for revenue and spending rules. Returning recoveries to their program keeps unpredictable receipts out of platform financing.

## Trade-off
Taxpayers receive no direct refund of recovered funds, and the anti-fraud office's results show up only in the programs it protects.
