## Question
How is the Dynamic Global Innovation Fund financed?

## Decision
A fixed statutory share of drug-price negotiation savings. UHI cost estimates count only the remainder.

## Why
Recycles pharmaceutical savings into breakthrough research; avoids drawing on the Reserve.
