## Question
How does the federal rent cap from decision 0014 actually work, and what is the platform's policy on homelessness after *Grants Pass*, Executive Order 14321, and HUD's attempts to cap permanent housing funding?

## Decision
- **R1 (mechanism): B + A.** A federal floor through conditions on federal benefits: covered landlords who exceed the cap lose accelerated and bonus depreciation and like-kind exchange deferral on that building, and federally backed multifamily loans carry the cap as a covenant. Incentive grants pay states and localities to extend caps to smaller landlords. No direct federal rent order. Penalty revenue goes to the General Fund and funds no program.
- **R2 (coverage): A.** Portfolios of 50 or more units, counting affiliates. Exempt: buildings within 15 years of first occupancy, owner-occupied buildings of one to four units, and units under a stricter federal subsidy cap. Separate from the Dominance Threshold.
- **R3 (rules): A.** The decision 0014 formula (inflation plus 5 percent, no more than 10 percent) applies during a tenancy, with vacancy reset; just-cause eviction; relocation assistance and a two-year anti-evasion rule after no-fault moves; fair-return petitions decided by administrative law judges within 90 days; fees count as rent; coordinated algorithmic pricing treated as a restraint of trade.
- **R4 (enforcement): all tools.** Tenant lawsuits with triple damages and fees, state attorneys general, annual rent-roll reporting with a mandatory IRS matching duty, no waivers.
- **H1: C.** Housing First as the statutory standard, with at least 70 percent of Continuum of Care funds for permanent housing, renewal of performing projects, a funding-notice deadline with automatic renewal, and notice and comment for any change in criteria. Abstinence-based recovery housing funded as a choice, never a precondition.
- **H2: C.** Voucher guarantee for extremely low-income households experiencing or at imminent risk of homelessness, paid from the General Fund in tier 2. Universal vouchers in tier 4. Exact order within each tier is set only by Section 27.9. Source-of-income discrimination prohibited.
- **H3: A.** An "adequate offer" condition on federal homelessness funds, with no criminal penalties for sleeping outside, storage of belongings, safety exceptions, and private and state enforcement.
- **H4: approved.** Due-process floor for any federally funded commitment; homelessness is never by itself grounds; crisis care through 988, mobile crisis teams, and stabilization centers paid by Universal Health Insurance; health records not shared with police without a warrant.
- **H5: approved.** Permanent emergency rental assistance with a capped automatic increase in recessions, right to counsel in evictions, and eviction-record sealing, paid from the General Fund in tier 2, in the order set by Section 27.9.

## Why
The rent-control evidence shows real supply costs, so the cap targets gouging on sitting tenants and leaves new construction and turnover free. Conditioning voluntary federal benefits gives a national floor without a takings fight. The evidence for Housing First is strong on housing stability and mixed on health, and the current administration has tried twice to reverse it administratively, so the floors and rules are written in statute.

## Revisions after review (Nov 13)
- Ordering within tiers is stated only in Section 27.9; Chapter 8 and this record refer to it.
- Section 8.3.1 gains a supportive-services standard (voluntary multidisciplinary teams; refusing services never costs housing).
- The adequate-offer rule ties accessibility to the reasonable-modification duties of the ADA and Section 504, as strengthened by Section 13.5.
- The chronic homelessness figure is sourced to CRS's summary of HUD's report.
