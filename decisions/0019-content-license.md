## Question
How are the platform and its data licensed?

## Decision
CC BY-ND 4.0 for platform and white-paper text; CC BY 4.0 for data, templates, decision records, and tooling.

## Why
Encourages quotation and sharing while preventing doctored versions under the author's name.
