## Question
What stable revenue funds recurring programs?

## Decision
A new Federal Tax Fairness chapter: capital gains taxed as ordinary income above a high threshold, restored top brackets, a stronger corporate minimum tax, and estate-tax reform. Rates set after JCT scoring.

## Why
Recurring obligations need stable revenue; the platform previously had no federal income-tax plank.
