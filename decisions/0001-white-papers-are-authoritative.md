## Question
Where the platform, website, or repository conflict with a white paper, which governs?

## Decision
The white paper governs. Chapters on health, AI, and Social Security, and matching website copy, are aligned to the papers.

## Why
Papers carry the most developed reasoning and the author's most recent decisions.
