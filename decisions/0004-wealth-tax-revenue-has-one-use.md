## Question
Where does Extreme Wealth Fair-Share Tax revenue go?

## Decision
It fills the Sovereign Wealth Reserve's stabilization balance to a statutory target; any excess goes to a Capital Investment Account for one-time projects only.

## Why
Wealth-tax revenue moves with markets, so it must not fund recurring obligations (Principle 6).
