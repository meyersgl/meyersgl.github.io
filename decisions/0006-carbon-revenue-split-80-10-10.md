## Question
How is carbon and methane fee revenue split so each dollar is counted once?

## Decision
80% equal per-person household dividend, 10% Transition Fund (sunsets), 10% General Fund limited to one-time or time-limited costs. Carbon border tariff receipts go to the household dividend.

## Why
Least reliance on a shrinking revenue base; border receipts to households avoid looking like a subsidy to protected industries; a large dividend answers the regressivity charge.
