**THE SOCIAL DEMOCRATIC MARKET PLATFORM**

**Government provides the foundation. Citizens provide the ambition. Markets provide the growth.**

The Social Democratic Market Platform rejects the false choice between centralized government control and unrestricted laissez-faire governance. It combines opportunity, growth, accountability, scientific progress, and fiscal responsibility into a practical governing framework.

**Opportunity**

Universal health insurance · Universal preschool · Tuition-free public higher education · Affordable housing

**Growth**

Competitive markets · Small business and entrepreneurship · Scientific research and innovation · Infrastructure modernization

**Accountability**

Anti-corruption reform · Campaign finance reform · Government transparency · Election security

**OUR PRINCIPLES**

✓ Opportunity should be universal.  
✓ Markets should be competitive.  
✓ Government should be accountable.  
✓ Science should inform policy.  
✓ Growth should be broadly shared.  
✓ Public investment should be fiscally responsible.

*Every program has a named, single-use funding source and an independent cost estimate before it takes effect.*

**Build the foundation. Expand opportunity. Strengthen democracy. Invest in the future. Empower the people.**
