Platform Document Repository

The Social Democratic Market Platform establishes an alternative governance track, rejecting the choice between completely unrestricted markets and direct centralized state management. This model leverages market competition for innovation and asset generation, while maintaining an unyielding public foundation for human security.

Core Policy Frameworks

Review the primary structural innovations across key sectors developed within the platform text.

🏥 Healthcare Infrastructure

  • Universal Coverage: Consolidated financing layer paired with independent clinical delivery models.
  • Innovation Fund: 7-year price-cap exemptions for pioneering treatments to protect clinical R&D.
  • Rural Floor: Volume-adjusted automated subsidies protecting thin-margin community networks.

👵 Social Security Solvency

  • Uncapped Subsidy: 6.2% tax levied on all earned wages exceeding $250,000 without expanding high-tier benefits.
  • Anti-Sheltering: Closes S-Corp pass-through and C-Corp retained cash shelters.
  • Sovereign Endowment: Channels wealth tax revenue into passive index funds.

🤖 Frontier AI Governance

  • Algorithmic Indexing: Replaces static hardware compute caps with dynamic vulnerability tests.
  • Stochastic Audits: Injects blind red-team probes into live streams to bypass model awareness.
  • Strict Liability: Places structural legal and civil accountability on developers for autonomous damage.

🌲 Climate & Energy

  • Pollution Dividend: Levies predictable source fees on carbon/methane, returning funds to consumers.
  • Border Protection: Closes manufacturing leakage via carbon-equalized import tariffs.
  • Grid Preemption: Grants FERC supreme siting rights for lines, bound to 180-day legal resolution limits.
