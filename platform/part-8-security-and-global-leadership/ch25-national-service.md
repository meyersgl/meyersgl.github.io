## Chapter 25: National Service

### 25.1 Guiding Principle

Strong nations are built by engaged citizens.

Government can provide opportunity.

Markets can create prosperity.

But communities thrive only when citizens actively contribute to the common good.

National service should strengthen civic trust, build skills, and foster shared responsibility.

Participation shall remain entirely voluntary.

### 25.2 National Service & Civic Engagement Act \[Statute\]

The federal government shall support voluntary service opportunities designed to strengthen communities while expanding educational and career opportunities.

The purpose of service is not compulsory labor.

The purpose is creating opportunities for citizens to contribute while developing skills and experience.

### 25.3 American Service Corps \[Statute\]

A voluntary national-service framework shall provide opportunities in:

- Disaster response

- Environmental conservation

- Infrastructure projects

- Education support

- Public-health outreach

- Elder care

- Community development

- Veterans assistance

Programs may operate through partnerships with:

- Nonprofits

- State governments

- Local governments

- Tribal governments

- Educational institutions

### 25.4 Service Scholarship Benefit \[Statute\]

Individuals successfully completing approved service terms shall receive flexible education and career benefits. Because tuition at public colleges, universities, and trade schools is covered under Section 7.3, benefits may be used for:

- Living costs while enrolled, such as housing, food, books, and transportation

- Apprenticeships and professional certifications

- Graduate or professional study

- Repayment of existing student loans

**Educational Opportunity Principle**

National service should expand opportunity rather than replace traditional educational pathways.

Cross-reference: See Chapter 7 Education.

### 25.5 Living Allowance Framework \[Statute\]

Full-time service participants shall receive:

- Living stipends

- Healthcare coverage through Universal Health Insurance (Chapter 5)

- Transportation support when necessary

- Housing assistance where appropriate

Compensation should support participation without transforming service into a conventional employment program.

### 25.6 Disaster Response Reserve Corps \[Statute\]

A specialized service track shall support Chapter 22 Disaster Resilience objectives.

Participants may receive training in:

- Emergency communications

- Shelter management

- Logistics support

- Disaster recovery

- Community resilience planning

Cross-reference: See Chapter 22 Disaster Resilience & Emergency Preparedness.

### 25.7 Conservation & Public Lands Corps \[Statute\]

Participants may contribute to projects involving:

- Reforestation

- Trail maintenance

- Habitat restoration

- Watershed improvement

- Wildfire mitigation

- Public-land stewardship

The program strengthens both environmental conservation and local economic development.

### 25.8 Community Health & Care Corps \[Statute\]

Participants may assist organizations involved in:

- Elder care

- Disability services

- Public-health outreach

- Community health initiatives

- Mental-health support programs

Service members shall supplement, not replace, trained professionals.

### 25.9 Infrastructure & Skilled Trades Track \[Statute\]

Service opportunities may include:

- Broadband deployment

- Housing rehabilitation

- Infrastructure maintenance

- Energy-efficiency projects

- Water-system upgrades

Participation may count toward apprenticeship requirements where appropriate.

Cross-reference: See Chapters 3 and 21.

### 25.10 Civic Leadership Fellowship Program \[Statute\]

Outstanding participants may compete for fellowships supporting careers in:

- Public administration

- Education

- Scientific research

- Emergency management

- Community development

The objective is cultivating future civic leaders.

### 25.11 National Unity Principle

National service should bring Americans together across:

- Geographic differences

- Economic backgrounds

- Cultural communities

- Political affiliations

The purpose is strengthening civic bonds and reinforcing the idea that citizenship carries both rights and responsibilities.
