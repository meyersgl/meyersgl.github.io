## Chapter 6: Reproductive Health Protection

### 6.1 Reproductive Health Protection \[Statute – High Resistance\]

Federal law shall protect access to reproductive healthcare, including contraception, abortion care, and pregnancy and postpartum care, nationwide.

Reproductive and maternal health services, including contraception, abortion care, and pregnancy and postpartum care, shall be covered under the public insurance system in Section 5.1.

#### 6.1.1 Abortion Care Is Health Care

Abortion care is women's health care. It shall be legal nationwide so that women can get the medical care they need.

The decision whether to have an abortion belongs to the woman and her doctor. Government shall not substitute its judgment for theirs, and medical decisions shall be guided by clinical standards of care, not by political interference.

#### 6.1.2 Federal Protection of Access

Federal law shall guarantee the legal right to abortion nationwide and shall preempt state laws that ban abortion or impose restrictions that are not medically necessary. Access to medication abortion, including by telehealth, shall be protected from restrictions that are not supported by medical evidence.

#### 6.1.3 Protection for Patients and Providers

Doctors and other clinicians shall be free to provide abortion care according to their medical judgment without threat of criminal or civil penalty. Patients shall not be criminalized for their pregnancy outcomes. Patients, providers, and those who assist them shall be protected from penalties imposed by another state for care that is lawful where it is provided.

#### 6.1.4 Coverage and Access

Abortion care shall be covered under the public insurance system in Section 5.1, and federal policy shall support access in areas with few providers.

#### 6.1.5 Honest Limit

Federal legislation can be repealed by a later Congress, and its constitutional basis will be challenged in court. Because this section leaves individual decisions to patients and their doctors rather than setting legislated limits, expect the sharpest opposition on later-term abortion.
