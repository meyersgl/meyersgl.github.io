## Chapter 22: Financing & Fiscal Sustainability

### 22.1 Guiding Principle

A platform is only as credible as its funding. Every major commitment in this document shall have an identified source of revenue, an independent cost estimate, and a realistic implementation sequence.

### 22.2 Funding Source Requirement \[Statute\]

Before any major new program in this platform takes effect, an independent cost estimate and an identified funding source shall be published. Programs shall be sequenced so that new obligations do not outpace identified revenue.

### 22.3 Revenue Sources \[Statute\]

Revenue to fund this platform shall come from:

- The Millionaire Fair-Share Tax (Chapter 1).

- Payroll tax cap and pass-through income reforms (Chapter 21).

- Carbon-fee revenue remaining after the household dividend (Chapter 15).

- Progressive income tax reform (Section 1.8).

- Recovery of fraud and improper payments (Section 7.4).

- Savings from consolidated health financing and negotiated rates, to the extent they materialize (Chapter 3).

### 22.4 Sovereign Wealth Reserve Priority Order \[Statute\]

Claims on the Sovereign Wealth Reserve and related revenue shall follow this order: (1) recession stabilization under Section 1.3; (2) a defined allocation to the Social Security endowment under Section 21.4; (3) a defined allocation to the Dynamic Global Innovation Fund under Section 3.3. Allocations under (2) and (3) shall be set in statute within annual caps and shall not reduce the stabilization balance below a statutory minimum.

### 22.5 Phased Implementation \[Statute\]

Programs shall be phased in by feasibility: first low-cost statutory reforms (such as voter registration, cannabis legalization, and anti-corruption measures), then revenue measures, and finally the largest new programs (health insurance, childcare, tuition-free higher education, K-12 equity funding, student loan reform, and expanded farm risk protection).

### 22.6 Independent Costing \[Statute\]

Cost estimates and revenue projections for each major program shall be developed by an independent nonpartisan body and published with implementing legislation. (Dollar estimates are not yet included in this edition.)
