## Chapter 13: Civil Rights & Equal Protection

### 13.1 Guiding Principle

Every person possesses inherent worth and deserves to be treated with dignity, fairness, and respect. Public institutions should safeguard individual rights, ensure due process, and maintain equal treatment under the law regardless of income, race, religion, sex, disability, age, political affiliation, or citizenship status (Governing Principle 1 and the Human Dignity Standard).

*Statutory provisions for this chapter (voting-rights enforcement, anti-discrimination protections, disability rights, and pay transparency) are scheduled for the v1.2 Complete Edition (decision 0003).*
