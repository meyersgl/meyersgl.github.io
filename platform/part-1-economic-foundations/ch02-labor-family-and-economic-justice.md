## CHAPTER 2: LABOR, FAMILY & ECONOMIC JUSTICE

### 2.1 Living Wage Framework \[Statute\]

The federal minimum wage shall be set as a fixed percentage of the federal poverty line and adjusted for regional cost of living using federal regional price data. To avoid sharp differences across metropolitan boundaries, the regional adjustment shall phase in gradually across a buffer zone (a linear taper over a defined distance) rather than changing at the boundary line.

### 2.2 Welfare Cliff Reform \[Statute\]

Benefit phase-outs shall occur gradually so additional work always increases net household income.

### 2.3 Protecting the Right to Unionize \[Statute\]

Workers shall have strong protections against retaliation and unfair labor practices, including faster union election timelines, limits on mandatory anti-union meetings, and meaningful penalties for illegal retaliation. Bargaining shall remain decentralized and voluntary at the firm level.

### 2.4 Strategic Domestic Production \[Statute\]

Critical emergency supplies shall maintain domestic production capacity. The list of critical supplies shall be narrow and statutorily defined, updated by an independent technical body rather than by industry petition, and aligned with the vulnerability reviews in Section 16.5 and the reserves in Section 17.6.

### 2.5 Paid Leave Framework \[Statute\]

All workers shall have access to paid family, parental, caregiving, and personal medical leave regardless of employer size, for up to 12 weeks per year. Wage replacement shall be funded by federal funds and administered as a social-insurance pool so that no single employer bears the full cost of a worker's leave. Job-protection requirements may be phased in for the smallest employers, but wage replacement shall be available to all workers.

### 2.6 Care Economy Infrastructure \[Statute – High Resistance\]

Childcare from birth to school entry shall have costs capped on a sliding scale relative to household income, with the cap phasing in smoothly rather than ending at an income cliff.

Public funds shall support provider pay, workforce training, and facility capacity so that affordable care is actually available, particularly for infants and in rural areas.

Eldercare costs shall likewise be capped on a sliding scale, and long-term care shall be addressed through the public insurance system under Section 3.2, with support for family caregivers.

### 2.7 Workforce Mobility & Occupational Licensing Reform \[Statute\]

Occupational licensing shall exist only where justified by public safety.

#### 2.7.1 Interstate License Recognition

Workers licensed in one state should generally be able to practice in another without repeating equivalent qualification requirements.

#### 2.7.2 Apprenticeship Expansion

Earn-while-you-learn career pathways shall be expanded through federal partnerships.

#### 2.7.3 Military Credential Recognition

Military certifications and experience shall receive expedited civilian recognition.
