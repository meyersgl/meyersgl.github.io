## Chapter 1: Macroeconomic Stability

### 1.1 Steady Economic Growth \[Statute\]

The central bank shall maintain a stable nominal GDP growth target of approximately 4-5 percent annually to reduce recession severity and maintain employment stability.

### 1.2 Recession Emergency Valve \[Statute\]

Baseline federal spending shall be capped relative to the size of the economy. The cap shall be suspended automatically, within the first quarter of a downturn, when a rules-based recession indicator (such as an early-warning unemployment trigger of the Sahm Rule type) is met and core inflation is at or below a defined target threshold. The inflation threshold prevents fiscal stimulus from compounding a supply-shock or stagflationary crisis. The cap shall return only after a sustained, multi-quarter confirmation of economic recovery, so that spending is not withdrawn prematurely. Outside a downturn, the cap may rise only by the independently scored revenue that a newly enacted program brings with it (Section 27.6).

### 1.3 Sovereign Wealth Reserve \[Statute\]

A protected sovereign reserve fund shall exist solely to stabilize the economy during recessions. It shall be funded by Extreme Wealth Fair-Share Tax revenue (Section 2.1) up to a stabilization target fixed in statute, drawn only when the recession trigger in Section 1.2 is met, and refilled after recovery. Revenue above the target shall go to a Capital Investment Account for one-time capital projects only. No other program, fund, or endowment may claim the Reserve, except the automatic extended unemployment benefits in Section 4.4.4. Full rules are in Section 27.5.
