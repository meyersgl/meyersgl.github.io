# APPENDIX A: CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENTS REQUIRED

## Purpose

Most policies contained within this platform may be enacted through federal legislation or executive action. Certain reforms, however, require amendment of the United States Constitution and therefore demand a substantially higher level of democratic consensus.

This appendix identifies all provisions within the platform that require constitutional amendment and summarizes their purpose.

The inclusion of a constitutional amendment proposal within this platform does not imply immediate enactment. Constitutional amendments should be pursued only through broad public support, democratic deliberation, and constitutional ratification procedures.

## Amendment 1: Federal Service Age Limits

Related Provision

Section 12.6 - Age Limit of 68

Proposal

No person shall run for or hold federal elected office after reaching age 68, and federal judges shall retire at age 68.

Purpose

The purpose of this amendment is to:

- Promote leadership renewal

- Expand generational representation

- Encourage long-term institutional adaptability

- Ensure regular opportunities for new leadership

Guiding Principle

Democratic institutions should remain responsive to changing generations while preserving institutional knowledge and stability.

## Amendment 2: Democratic Election Finance Reform

Related Provision

Section 12.7 - Campaign Finance & Anti-Corruption

Proposal

Congress and the States shall have the authority to establish reasonable limits on independent political expenditures and campaign financing while preserving:

- Freedom of speech

- Freedom of the press

- Freedom of association

- Legitimate issue advocacy

Purpose

The purpose of this amendment is to:

- Reduce undue financial influence in elections

- Increase public confidence in democratic institutions

- Strengthen political equality

- Enhance transparency and accountability

Guiding Principle

Political influence should derive primarily from citizens rather than the concentration of financial resources.

## Future Constitutional Reform Considerations

The following subjects may warrant future national discussion but are not currently proposed as constitutional amendments within this platform:

- Electoral College reform

- Presidential term structure

- Expansion of voting rights protections

- Congressional representation reform

- Constitutional ethics requirements for federal officeholders

- Constitutional protection for the independence of federal investigations and prosecutions (pursued by statute in Section 15.11; an amendment would be considered only if courts reject that approach)

These issues would require additional public debate and are not incorporated as active amendment proposals within this edition of the platform.

## Constitutional Amendment Principles

Constitutional amendments should satisfy the following standards:

> Necessity
>
> Amendments should be pursued only when statutory solutions are insufficient.
>
> Democratic Legitimacy
>
> Amendments should reflect broad and durable public support across political, geographic, and demographic boundaries.
>
> Preservation of Liberty
>
> No amendment should weaken fundamental constitutional rights, civil liberties, or democratic protections.
>
> Institutional Stability

Amendments should strengthen the long-term effectiveness, legitimacy, and resilience of American constitutional government.

## Constitutional Governance Statement

The Constitution of the United States provides the framework within which democratic self-government operates.

This platform seeks reform through constitutional processes, democratic consensus, and the rule of law.

Constitutional amendments should be rare, carefully considered, and adopted only when necessary to strengthen democratic accountability, equal opportunity, human dignity, and the long-term resilience of the Republic.

The Constitution establishes the framework. Democratic institutions provide accountability. Citizens provide legitimacy. Future generations inherit the results.
