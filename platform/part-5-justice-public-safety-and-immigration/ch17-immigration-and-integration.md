## Chapter 17: Immigration & Integration

### 17.1 Guiding Principle

The United States is both a sovereign nation and a nation of immigrants.

A successful immigration system must balance:

- Border security

- Rule of law

- Economic needs

- Human dignity

- National security

- Individual opportunity

The objective is not merely controlling migration. The objective is creating an immigration system that is secure, orderly, efficient, and humane.

### 17.2 Immigration Reorganization Framework \[Statute\]

Federal immigration enforcement shall be reorganized to separate criminal enforcement from civil immigration administration.

The Immigration and Customs Enforcement agency shall be dissolved in its current form, and its functions shall be reassigned as described below.

**Criminal Enforcement Division**

Specialized units shall focus on:

- Human trafficking

- Cartels

- Organized smuggling

- Transnational criminal activity

**Civil Immigration Administration**

Administrative functions shall transition to a professional civilian agency emphasizing:

- Efficiency

- Due process

- Customer service

- Legal compliance

The objective is improved effectiveness without weakening enforcement.

### 17.3 Immigration & National Integration Act \[Statute\]

Federal immigration policy shall be structured around a simple principle:

Individuals seeking a better life should have a safe, lawful, and achievable path to enter the United States, contribute to society, and pursue opportunity.

#### 17.3.1 Secure, Orderly & Humane Framework

Border security remains a legitimate responsibility of government.

However, enforcement efforts should prioritize:

- Criminal organizations

- Human trafficking networks

- Smuggling enterprises

- National security threats

rather than treating all migrants as criminal actors.

#### 17.3.2 Legal Pathway Modernization

The current immigration system frequently creates unnecessary delays and bureaucratic complexity.

Federal reforms shall:

- Reduce processing delays

- Modernize application systems

- Expand staffing capacity

- Streamline verification procedures

- Improve transparency

The objective is making lawful immigration significantly easier than unlawful immigration.

#### 17.3.3 Workforce & Economic Immigration

Legal immigration pathways shall be expanded in sectors experiencing documented labor shortages.

Priority occupations may include:

- Healthcare

- Engineering

- Scientific research

- Skilled trades

- Advanced manufacturing

- Technology

- Education

Cross-reference: See Section 3.5 Workforce Mobility & Occupational Licensing Reform.

#### 17.3.4 Agricultural & Seasonal Worker Programs

Agricultural production depends heavily on seasonal labor.

The federal government shall maintain accessible guest-worker pathways for:

- Agricultural labor

- Seasonal industries

- Food-production systems

while enforcing:

- Worker protections

- Wage standards

- Safe working conditions

Cross-reference: See Chapter 10 Agriculture & Family Farms.

#### 17.3.5 Dreamers, Long-Term Residents & Citizenship Pathways

Individuals brought to the United States as children should have access to a streamlined pathway to lawful status and eventual citizenship.

Priority shall also be given to long-term residents who:

- Maintain clean records

- Demonstrate economic participation

- Meet legal requirements

Priority shall be given to lawful pathways to status and eventual citizenship for Dreamers, agricultural workers, and long-term productive residents, upon satisfaction of established legal requirements.

### 17.4 Integration & Civic Participation \[Statute\]

Immigration success depends upon integration as well as admission.

Federal programs shall support:

- English-language instruction

- Workforce development

- Civic education

- Credential recognition

- Community participation

The goal is helping new Americans become successful contributors to their communities.

### 17.5 Human Dignity Principle

Every person interacting with America's immigration system shall be treated with:

- Fairness

- Respect

- Due process

- Human dignity

Government should enforce immigration law without abandoning fundamental humanitarian values.
