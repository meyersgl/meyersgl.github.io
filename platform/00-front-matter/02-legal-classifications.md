# Legal Classifications

**\[Statute\]** Federal legislation passed by majorities in both chambers. In the Senate, ending debate currently requires 60 votes, unless the provision is enacted through budget reconciliation or after the Senate rules reform in Appendix B. The legislative path for each plank is listed in Appendix B, Section B.3.

**\[Statute – High Resistance\]** Legislation facing substantial political, legal, economic, or administrative barriers.

**\[Senate Rule\]** A change to the Senate's standing rules or precedents, adopted by the Senate alone (Appendix B).

**\[Executive\]** Achievable through executive authority.

**\[Constitutional Amendment\]** Requires constitutional ratification.

**Constitutionally Contested** A flag added to a classification when serious constitutional objections are likely. Each such provision includes an honest-limit note.
