# AUTHOR'S NOTE

The Social Democratic Market Platform was developed by Gregory L. Meyers through an iterative process of research, analysis, drafting, review, and revision.

Various software tools, including artificial intelligence-assisted drafting, editing, and research-support tools, were used during portions of the development process to improve organization, clarity, and presentation. All policy positions, structural decisions, classifications, revisions, and final content reflect the judgment, review, and approval of the author.

This document represents the author's policy recommendations and governing philosophy. Its purpose is to provide a comprehensive framework for public discussion concerning economic opportunity, democratic accountability, competitive markets, scientific progress, fiscal responsibility, and long-term national resilience.
