# Scope of This Edition

This edition sets out the platform's positions, legal design, financing rules, and legislative strategy. Some topics need more detail than a platform chapter can carry. They are handled in standalone white papers, which govern where they and the platform differ (decision 0001).

## Published White Papers

| White paper | Platform chapter |
| --- | --- |
| *Universal Health Insurance: A Hybrid Framework for Universal Coverage* | 5 |
| *Social Security Solvency: Without Benefit Cuts or Raising the Retirement Age* | 26 |
| *Frontier AI Governance: A Framework for Innovation, Accountability, and Public Safety* | 19 |

## What This Edition Does Not Yet Include

- **Dollar estimates.** No program has been independently scored. The costing table is maintained in `data/costing.csv` and will be filled from published estimates (Section 27.12).
- **Implementing legislation.** Sections describe what the law shall do, not its statutory text.
- **Financial regulation** and **region-specific foreign policy**, which await the white papers above.
