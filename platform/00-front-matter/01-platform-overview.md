# Platform Overview

**Government provides the foundation. Citizens provide the ambition. Markets provide the growth.**

The Social Democratic Market Platform rejects the choice between centralized economic control and unrestricted markets. Government guarantees a strong public foundation: healthcare, education, infrastructure, public safety, democratic institutions, and equal opportunity. On that foundation, competitive markets remain the primary engine of innovation, investment, and prosperity.

## Three Core Goals

1. Guarantee every American a baseline of economic security and genuine opportunity.
2. Preserve competitive markets, entrepreneurship, private property, and free enterprise as the primary engines of innovation, growth, and wealth creation.
3. Use government intervention only where markets systematically fail to provide efficient, fair, or broadly accessible outcomes.

## How the Platform Is Organized

| Part | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| I. Economic Foundations | Rules-based recession response, federal tax fairness including the Extreme Wealth Fair-Share Tax, a living wage, paid leave and care, and the safety net |
| II. Human Development | Universal Health Insurance, reproductive health protection, education from preschool through tuition-free public college, and housing |
| III. Competitive Markets | Anti-monopoly enforcement and consumer rights, family farms, and trade with strategic protection |
| IV. Democracy, Governance & Public Trust | Election reform, civil rights, effective government, and ethics |
| V. Justice, Public Safety & Immigration | Violent-crime priority, rehabilitation, accountability, and a secure, orderly, humane immigration system |
| VI. Science & Technology | Research and space leadership, and capability-based frontier AI governance |
| VII. Climate, Infrastructure & Resilience | A carbon fee with household dividends, clean-energy buildout, infrastructure and supply chains, and disaster resilience |
| VIII. Security & Global Leadership | Diplomacy first, strong alliances, a capable defensive military, and voluntary national service |
| IX. Long-Term Sustainability | Social Security without benefit cuts, and the Master Financing Framework |

## How It Is Paid For

Every revenue stream is assigned to exactly one use, so no dollar is counted twice, and recurring programs draw only on stable revenue (Chapter 27). No major program takes effect without an independent cost estimate and its assigned revenue. Dollar estimates are not yet included in this edition.

## How to Read It

Each provision carries a legal classification (see Legal Classifications), and the most difficult provisions include an *honest limit* stating their main legal or political risk. Where the platform and a white paper differ, the white paper governs. A full Executive Summary is published separately.
